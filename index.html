Do I need a repair or a full replacement?

We start with a diagnostic. If only a few modules are weak, a repair may save money. If the pack is heavily degraded, a full replacement is usually the safer long-term option. You get the recommendation and the price before any work.

What does the $150 diagnostic include?

We come to your location, scan the hybrid system, check the battery module by module and explain the options. If you go ahead with repair or replacement, the $150 is credited in full, so the diagnostic costs you $0. If you decide not to, the visit is $150.

How is a repair priced?

Repair starts at $450 plus the cells your pack needs. The diagnostic shows how many cells are weak, and we confirm the total with you before starting.

How fast can you come?

Same-day or next-day visits are often possible in LA, Orange County and the South Bay, depending on the schedule and your location.

Which areas do you cover?

We regularly work across Los Angeles County, Orange County, the South Bay and parts of Ventura County. Availability depends on your city and our schedule, so text your city to confirm.

Do you offer a warranty?

Full replacements include a 1-year warranty. Repair warranty depends on the condition of the rest of the pack and the diagnostic result, and we confirm it before any work.

Do you only work on Prius?

Prius is our main focus, but we also support select Toyota and Lexus hybrid models. Text your year and model to confirm.

Can you come to my driveway, apartment or workplace?

Yes. We are mobile and can usually service the vehicle at your home, apartment, workplace or another safe parking spot.

Is it the hybrid battery or the 12V battery?

Text us the warning light, code or symptom. We help separate hybrid battery, 12V battery and other issues before scheduling a visit.
