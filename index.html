FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need repair or full replacement?

We start with a diagnostic. If only a few modules are weak, repair may save money. If the pack is heavily degraded, full replacement is usually the safer long-term option.

What does the diagnostic include?

We come to your location, scan the hybrid system, check the battery at module level, and explain the repair path. If you move forward, the diagnostic is credited toward the service.

How fast can you come?

Same-day or next-day availability is often possible in LA, Orange County, and the South Bay depending on schedule and location.

Do you offer warranty?

Full replacements include a 1-year warranty. Repair warranty depends on the condition of the existing battery pack and the diagnostic result.

Do you only work on Prius?

Prius is our main focus, but we also support select Toyota and Lexus hybrid models. Text your year and model to confirm.

Can you come to my driveway or apartment?

Yes. We are mobile. We can usually service the vehicle at your home, apartment, workplace, or another safe parking location.
