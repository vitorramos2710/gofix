Hybrid Battery Service Process

Diagnose. Repair. Verify.

A simple three-step process built for Prius and Toyota/Lexus NiMH hybrid packs — diagnose first, then choose repair or replacement.

Cell-level diagnostic
We scan and load-test every module in the pack to find the weak cells — not just the pack as a whole.
Repair or full replacement
If only a few modules are weak, repair may save money. If the pack is too degraded, full replacement is the safer long-term option.
Road-test & verification
We clear codes, verify the hybrid system, and confirm the car is ready. Full replacements include a 1-year warranty.
